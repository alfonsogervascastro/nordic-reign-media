**YouTube title:** Vikings Never Wore Horned Helmets. Here's Who Did | #shorts

**Caption:**
No Viking Age helmet with horns has ever been found. The only near-complete one, from Gjermundbu in Norway (10th century), has a rounded cap and a spectacle guard. The horns came in 1876, when costume designer Carl Emil Doepler dressed the cast of Wagner's Ring cycle. Horned helmets did exist, in the Nordic Bronze Age, around 1,700 years before the Vikings.
